12-Week Progression Plan

Phase 1
Foundation & Adaptation
Weeks 1-4
  • Intensity: 65-75% 1RM / RPE 7
  • Volume: Moderate - master technique first
  • Compound reps: 6-8; Accessory: 10-15
  • Add 2.5kg when RPE stays at or below 7 for 2 sessions
  • Log every session: weight, reps, and RPE
  • Rest: 2-3 min compounds, 90 sec accessories
Phase 2
Strength & Hypertrophy
Weeks 5-8
  • Intensity: 75-85% 1RM / RPE 8
  • Volume: Increase by 1 set per main compound
  • Compound reps: 4-6; Accessory: 8-12
  • Increase 2.5-5kg when top rep range hit 2 sessions in a row
  • Introduce 1 drop set per session on final accessory
  • Rest: 3 min compounds, 90-120 sec accessories
Phase 3
Intensification & Peak
Weeks 9-12
  • Intensity: 80-90% 1RM / RPE 8-9
  • Volume: Slightly reduced sets, heavier loads
  • Compound reps: 3-5; Accessory: 8-10
  • Double progression: increase load or reps each session
  • Week 11: Full deload (see below)
  • Week 12: Test new 1RM or top rep set on key lifts
Deload Protocol - Week 11

Reduce volume by 40-50% and load by 20%. Perform all exercises with perfect technique at a controlled tempo. Do NOT skip the deload - this is when your body consolidates adaptations, heals micro-traumas, and resets the nervous system. On carnivore, the deload week is especially powerful as systemic inflammation drops sharply. Use this week to focus on sleep quality and full dietary compliance.

  • Tuna (moderate frequency due to mercury)
  • Oysters (1-2x/week) - zinc, copper, selenium

Electrolyte Strategy - Whole Foods Only

On carnivore, low insulin causes kidneys to excrete sodium, potassium, and magnesium at higher rates. Deficiency causes fatigue, cramps, and brain fog often mistaken for adaptation problems.

Sodium — 2,000-4,000mg/day
Salt all meat liberally. Use pink Himalayan or sea salt on every meal. Homemade bone broth is excellent. Do not fear salt on carnivore.
Potassium — 3,000-4,700mg/day
Ribeye steak, salmon, sardines, and beef heart are all high in potassium. Eating 600-700g of meat daily covers this naturally.
Magnesium — 300-400mg/day
Beef liver, mackerel, and oysters are the best whole-food sources. Prioritize seafood 3-4x per week to cover this without supplements.
Hydration: Target 3-4 litres of water per day. Carnivore is naturally diuretic so drink to thirst plus an extra 500ml. Morning: 500ml water with a generous pinch of salt before training. Bone broth (1-2 cups per day) is ideal for hydration, electrolytes, and collagen for joint health.

Dirty to Strict Carnivore Transition

A gradual transition prevents GI distress, cravings, and adaptation fatigue. The 12-week training plan aligns with progressive dietary strictness so both training and nutrition peak together.

1
Weeks 1-2: Remove the Worst Offenders
Eliminate all processed junk, seed oils (canola, sunflower, soybean), refined sugars, packaged snacks, and fast food. You can still have coffee, dairy (butter, cheese, heavy cream), and minor condiments. Increase meat and egg portions to replace eliminated calories. Target 80%+ of calories from animal foods this fortnight.
2
Weeks 3-4: Plant Food Reduction Phase
Begin removing plant foods systematically. Start with grains, legumes, and high-oxalate vegetables (spinach, almonds, chocolate). If eating salads or cooked vegetables, reduce to 10% of each meal then eliminate by end of Week 4. Keep fermented dairy if gut health requires transition support. Monitor energy, sleep, and gym performance - these should stabilize or improve.
3
Weeks 5-6: Dairy Evaluation Phase
Remove all dairy except butter, which is nearly pure fat with minimal protein and casein. Evaluate whether heavy cream or cheese was causing bloating or stalled fat loss. Many people perform best with butter only. If fat loss is progressing and performance is strong, small amounts of hard cheese (aged cheddar, parmesan) can remain temporarily.
4
Weeks 7-8: Coffee Elimination Phase
Coffee is a plant-derived compound - strict carnivore excludes it. Begin tapering from Weeks 7-8. Reduce gradually: 2 cups to 1 cup to half a cup to none over 2 weeks. This prevents caffeine withdrawal headaches from disrupting training. If energy remains stable without coffee, you have achieved full fat-adaptation. If pre-workout performance drops sharply, keep 1 black coffee before training temporarily.
5
Weeks 9-12: Full Strict Carnivore
Diet consists exclusively of meat, fish, eggs, animal fats, organ meats, water, and salt. This is when the deepest metabolic benefits compound - anti-inflammatory cascade, peak insulin sensitivity, maximum fat oxidation. Your body is fully fat-adapted and using fatty acids as the primary fuel for both daily life and training. Performance should be stable and strength should be increasing week over week.
Managing the Carnivore Adaptation Period (Weeks 1-3): Expect 3-10 days of fatigue, brain fog, or mild headaches as metabolism switches from glucose to fat. Combat this by: salting all food aggressively, drinking bone broth daily, increasing fat content in meals (ribeye over chicken breast), and sleeping an extra 30-60 minutes per night. This is temporary and is a sign of metabolic transition, not a problem with the approach.

Recovery and Lifestyle

🌙
Sleep Protocol
  • Target 7.5-9 hours of sleep - muscle growth happens here, not in the gym
  • Consistent bed and wake time within 30 minutes, 7 days per week
  • Room temperature 18-20 degrees Celsius for deeper sleep architecture
  • No screens 60 minutes before bed - blue light suppresses melatonin
  • Last meal 2-3 hours before sleep to optimize growth hormone release
  • Carnivore advantage: tryptophan and glycine from meat are natural sleep aids
🔁
Active Recovery and Mobility
  • Thursday: 20-30 min easy walk Zone 1-2 - do not skip this session
  • Daily: 5-10 min hip flexor and thoracic spine mobility (cumulative benefit)
  • Post-leg day: 2 min pigeon pose per side and 2 min couch stretch per side
  • Post-push day: 2 min doorway chest stretch and pec minor release
  • Post-pull day: 2 min lat stretch overhead and shoulder cross-body stretch
  • Weekly: 10 min foam roll on quads, thoracic spine, and lats
🥩
Training on Carnivore — Key Tips
  • Train 2-3 hours after your first meat meal, not fully fasted in early weeks
  • If energy is low in Weeks 1-3, add extra butter or tallow to pre-workout meal
  • Fat adaptation completes around Week 4-6 and energy becomes rock solid
  • Do not measure peak performance in Weeks 1-3 during transition
  • Post-workout: prioritize protein within 90 minutes - steak or ground beef
  • Expect a 1-2 week strength stall at fat-adaptation then a strong rebound
🛡️
Injury Prevention
  • Always perform 2 warm-up sets before first working set on compound lifts
  • Never skip the 10 minute warm-up routine - this is non-negotiable
  • For knees: terminal knee extensions before every squat and leg day
  • For shoulders: band pull-aparts 3 sets of 20 before every push session
  • For lower back: hip hinge practice with light load before every deadlift session
  • If joint pain occurs: reduce load 20%, address technique, add face pulls daily
📊
Progress Tracking
  • Weigh 3x per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday) morning post-void
  • Use 7-day rolling average - daily fluctuations on carnivore are significant
  • Take progress photos every 2 weeks in same lighting at same time of day
  • Track every session in a training log - this is required for progressive overload
  • Target rate: 0.5-1% bodyweight per week loss (0.4-0.8kg per week at 82kg)
  • Losing more than 1kg per week: add 200 kcal per day from fat sources
⚡
Metabolic Health Optimization
  • Morning sunlight: 5-10 minutes outdoor exposure to reset cortisol rhythm
  • Stress management: chronic cortisol directly opposes body recomposition goals
  • Cold exposure optional: cold shower post-training reduces delayed onset muscle soreness
  • Eliminate alcohol entirely - it suppresses testosterone and severely disrupts sleep
  • Intermittent fasting optional: 16:8 eating window pairs naturally with carnivore
  • Organ meats naturally support liver function, the central hub of metabolic health
Realistic Recomposition Expectations at 34: As an intermediate lifter following this program, expect 1-2 kg of lean muscle gain and 4-6 kg of fat loss over 12 weeks. These are conservative, sustainable, and real numbers based on what physiology supports. The combination of PPL training with progressive overload, high-protein carnivore nutrition, and prioritized sleep and recovery is one of the most evidence-supported approaches for simultaneous fat loss and muscle gain at your age and experience level. Trust the process, log everything, and stay consistent.
