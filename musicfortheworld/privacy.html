Your privacy matters. This page explains, in plain words, what the musicfortheworld_ Autoposter touches and what it does not.

1Overview

musicfortheworld_ Autoposter (the “App”) is a small personal automation tool. It is used by its owner only, to publish the owner’s own original music videos to the owner’s own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and Twitch.

The App is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users, and it does not collect information about visitors or third parties.

2Information the App accesses

When the owner connects an account, the App receives only what is needed to publish content:

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner’s channel (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel details such as the channel ID (youtube.readonly), so the App can confirm it is posting to the correct channel.
  • Instagram (Meta Graph API): permission to publish Reels to the owner’s own Business or Creator account.
  • Twitch: permission to update the owner’s channel title and to stream to the owner’s own channel.
  • Video files: music videos from the owner’s own library, which are sent to the platforms above.

The App does not read, download or analyse anyone’s personal data, comments, subscribers, messages or viewing history.

3How the information is used

Access is used for one purpose only: uploading and publishing the owner’s content on a schedule, and confirming that the correct account is being used. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

4Storage and security

  • Access tokens and API credentials are stored in a private configuration file on the owner’s own computer or server. They are never published in a public repository or on this website.
  • The App keeps a simple local log of file names and posting status (success or failure) so that it does not post the same video twice.
  • No database of personal information is maintained.

5Sharing and disclosure

The App does not sell, rent, trade or share any information with anyone. The only data that leaves the owner’s system is the content being published, which goes directly to YouTube, Instagram or Twitch.

6Google API Services & YouTube API Services

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7Retention and revoking access

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use. Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove the App.
  • Instagram: remove the App under Settings → Apps and Websites.
  • Twitch: disconnect it under Settings → Connections.

The owner can also delete the stored tokens from the private configuration file at any time.

8Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
